# XoX Philosophy

## Core Identity
XoX is the developer-focused product derived from mature XoXLang semantics. XoXLang remains the scientific semantic authority and continues to evolve independently. XoX adopts only sufficiently mature rules.

## Foundational Principles

1. **Monotonic Guarantees**: More access never means fewer guarantees.
2. **Internalized Complexity**: Complex guarantees remain inside the engine until their complexity is necessary.
3. **Developer-First Sophistication**: Sophistication must serve the developer, not be imposed on them.
4. **Decisional Integrity**: Ease of use must never remove information required for a correct decision.
5. **Accidental Misuse Prevention**: XoX must be difficult to misuse accidentally, not merely possible to use correctly.
6. **Consequence Transparency**: Hiding mechanisms is acceptable, but hiding decision-relevant consequences is not.
7. **Justified Surface**: Public complexity must be justified by a guarantee, a capability, or a measurable risk reduction.
8. **Proportional Conceptual Footprint**: XoX must remain conceptually smaller than the problem it solves.
